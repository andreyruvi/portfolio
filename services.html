Services

What I can take off your desk

From a single permit set to ongoing documentation support. Every engagement runs against your standards — your title block, your layers, your view templates.

01

Architectural BIM Modeling

Revit models built to a defined level of development, structured so the drawings, schedules and views come out of the model rather than being drawn twice.

  • Revit architectural modelling to agreed LOD
  • Sheet set-up, views and view templates
  • Door, window and room schedules
  • Family creation and parametric components
  • Model auditing and clean-up of inherited files
02

Permit Drawings

Residential and commercial permit sets prepared to submit — coordinated, annotated and code-aware, with revisions turned around quickly.

  • Cover sheet, index and general notes
  • Floor plans, elevations, sections and details
  • Site plans, setbacks and coverage analysis
  • Schedules and code compliance annotation
  • Revision cycles through plan check
03

CAD Drafting

Precise AutoCAD drafting from sketches, markups, PDFs or field measurements, delivered on your layer standard and title block.

  • Redraw from sketch, PDF or scan
  • Working to your layer and title block standards
  • Dimensioning, annotation and detailing
  • PDF-to-CAD and CAD clean-up
  • DWG and PDF deliverables
04

Construction Documentation

Full CD sets that a builder can actually work from — framing, details and building services coordinated against the architectural set.

  • Framing plans and structural layouts
  • Wall, roof and connection details
  • Electrical, lighting and HVAC layouts
  • Schedules and specification annotation
  • Coordination between disciplines
05

Scan-to-BIM and As-Built

Point cloud data turned into a dependable existing-conditions model, so renovation and refurbishment design starts from what is actually there.

  • Point cloud registration and clean-up
  • Existing-conditions Revit modelling
  • Deviation checking against the scan
  • Measured elevations and sections
  • Heritage and irregular geometry
06

Structural Drafting

Structural plans, framing layouts and connection details for concrete, steel and timber, coordinated with the architectural model.

  • Foundation and framing plans
  • Concrete, steel and timber detailing
  • Reinforcement and connection details
  • Structural BIM modelling in Revit
  • Coordination with the architectural set
07

MEP Modeling and Coordination

Mechanical, electrical and plumbing modelling and clash coordination, run against the architectural and structural models before it reaches site.

  • HVAC, electrical and plumbing modelling
  • Equipment layouts and service routing
  • Clash detection and resolution tracking
  • Fire protection layouts
  • Coordinated drawings and schedules
08

3D Modeling and Visualisation

SketchUp and Revit models for design review, marketing or planning submission, with rendered views when the drawings need to persuade as well as inform.

  • SketchUp and Revit modelling
  • Interior and exterior rendering
  • Site and context modelling
  • Massing studies and design options
  • Presentation views and walkthrough stills
